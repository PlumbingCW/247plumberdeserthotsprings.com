This policy explains what information Desert Hot Springs 24/7 Plumbing collects when you use this website or call our dispatch line, and how we use it.

Call recording notice

Calls to our dispatch line may be recorded and monitored for quality, training and to confirm the details of your request. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call, and we will note the details by other means where we can.

Information we collect

This website has no forms and does not ask you to enter personal details. When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of your plumbing problem so we can schedule the visit. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write.

How we use information

We use your details to schedule and complete service, to contact you about your appointment, and to keep records of work performed. We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and cookies

This site is a static website and does not set advertising cookies of its own. Our hosting and any analytics we enable may collect basic technical data, such as page views and browser type, in aggregate form.

Sharing

We may share information with the technicians who service your address, with payment processors if you pay electronically and where the law requires it. We do not share call details with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing contact@247plumberdeserthotsprings.com. California residents may have additional rights under state privacy law, and we will respond to requests as the law requires.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@247plumberdeserthotsprings.com. This policy was last updated on November 14, 2026.
