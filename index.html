When To Call

What counts as a plumbing emergency

An emergency plumber visit is worth it when the problem cannot wait for morning. That means water you cannot stop, sewage backing into the house, a leaking gas water heater, no water at all, or a pipe that has burst. If you are unsure, call anyway. We will tell you plainly whether it can wait.

If water will not stop

Shut off the main water valveIt is usually near the meter or where the line enters the house. Turn it until the flow stops.
Switch off power near the waterOnly if you can reach the breaker without stepping in water.
Stop using drainsIf sewage is backing up, every fixture feeds the same pipe, so hold off on showers, laundry and flushing.

Local Knowledge

Plumbing in Desert Hot Springs is its own thing

Desert Hot Springs sits on the north edge of the Coachella Valley, along Palm Drive and Pierson Boulevard, which centre the original one square mile townsite. Homes near that core are among the oldest in the city, and older homes tend to have older pipe, so a repeated clog or a pinhole leak there is worth investigating rather than patching.

The city is known for its hot mineral water and spa district, home to businesses such as Two Bunch Palms and Miracle Manor Retreat, which depend on hot water and clean drains every day. Mineral buildup and heavy hot water use wear fixtures faster, so a water heater that rumbles or a faucet crusted with scale is a maintenance problem as much as a repair one.

Newer subdivisions spread toward Dillon Road, Mission Springs Park on Palm Drive, and Wardman Park on 8th Street, and each has its own mix of slab foundations, garage water heaters and outdoor irrigation lines. Desert sun cracks hose bibs and exposed fittings, and the Mission Creek Fault runs through the area, which is why we check heater bracing and shutoff valves on visits.

The northern part of the city is also in transition. Mission Springs Water District is running its M2 Septic to Sewer Project for about 695 properties, between San Gorgonio Street, Dillon Road, Yerxa Road and Avenida Gracia. If your home is in or near that area, tell the dispatcher whether you are on septic or sewer before we cable a drain or dig.
