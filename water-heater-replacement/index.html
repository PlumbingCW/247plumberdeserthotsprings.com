How to tell a heater is past saving

Water at the base of the tank

Seepage from the tank body means the steel liner has failed. Fittings can be repaired, the tank cannot.

Rusty or brown hot water

Rust only on the hot side points to a corroded tank, not to the pipes.

Age near the end of its range

Many tank heaters last about eight to twelve years. Check the date on the data label.

Frequent repairs

A second or third repair in a year usually costs more than the unit is worth.

Loud popping or rumbling

Heavy sediment makes the burner work harder and shortens tank life.

Not enough hot water

A tank that no longer keeps up often has a failing element, a thick sediment layer or both.

What a replacement visit includes

1

Shut off and drain

We close the water and gas or power, then drain the old tank safely.

2

Remove and haul away

The old heater is disconnected and taken out of the house.

3

Set and connect the new unit

We place the heater, connect water and gas or power, and install a new relief valve and discharge line.

4

Test and explain

We check for leaks, verify the burner or elements and set the temperature, then show you the shutoff valves.

A replacement is also the moment to fix what the old heater was hiding. We check the vent on a gas unit, look at the water pressure and add an expansion tank when the system needs one. We also confirm the earthquake bracing, since strapping a tank heater is standard practice in earthquake country and Desert Hot Springs sits on the Mission Creek Fault.

Sizing a heater for a Desert Hot Springs home

Size follows how many people use hot water at the same time. A couple in a small home does well with a smaller tank, while a family with back to back showers needs more recovery capacity. Many homes here are single story with the heater in a garage, and some vacation rentals see heavy use on weekends, so we size to your busiest day, not your quietest.

Manufactured and mobile homes use different heater models and clearances, and some park rules limit what can be installed. Tell the dispatcher what type of home you have so the right unit is on the truck. For work that is mostly about the new install itself, see water heater installation.

Permits and code

The City of Desert Hot Springs Building Division handles permits, and the California Plumbing Code governs water heater installation. Replacing a water heater is normally permitted work, and we can walk you through what the division asks for. You can reach the division at (760) 329-6411 if you want to confirm your case.

A replacement is set to the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), covering the relief valve, discharge line, venting and gas connection.
