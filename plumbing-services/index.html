How to choose a service page

Start with the symptom, not the pipe. A slow drain and a sewage backup are different problems, and a warm floor spot and a high water bill can both point to a leak under the slab. If you are unsure, call and describe what you see. The dispatcher will point you to the right visit, and tell you if it can wait until morning.

Many problems have a quick fix and a lasting fix. A cable opens a clogged line, while hydro jetting cleans it. A patch stops a pinhole leak, while repiping ends a pattern of them. Each page explains the difference so you can decide with the facts in front of you.

If you manage a rental, a restaurant or a multi-unit building, start with commercial plumbing. It covers after-hours access, grease lines and written summaries for owners. Homeowners with an older house should read repiping and sewer camera inspection, because older pipe is the most common reason a small repair turns into a repeat call.

All of our work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

For problems in progress, skip the reading and call the 24/7 line, since the dispatcher can tell you what to shut off while you wait.

Location matters too. Palm Springs and Desert Hot Springs are close, but their sewer systems, water providers and building departments are different, so a good plumber knows which office to call. Our Palm Springs page explains the differences for that city.

Every page also explains what shapes the price, because the honest answer to "how much" is "it depends on what we find." You hear a clear price before work starts, and we explain any change before we proceed.
