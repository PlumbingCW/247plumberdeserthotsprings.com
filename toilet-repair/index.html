Running toilets and high water bills

A toilet that quietly runs can waste a lot of water every day, and it shows up as a higher bill, not a visible leak. A quick test is to put a few drops of food coloring in the tank and wait without flushing. If color appears in the bowl, the flapper is leaking. Fixing it is inexpensive, and it is one of the most common ways to stop a bill from climbing.

If the bill is high and the toilets are fine, the problem may be elsewhere. Our leak detection page explains the meter test we use.

When we repair and when we replace

Most toilets can be repaired with new internal parts. We replace the toilet when the bowl is cracked, the porcelain is chipped near the seat, the flush is chronically weak, or the model is old enough that parts are scarce. Older toilets from the early decades of the city can use much more water per flush than current models, so a replacement can lower your bill.

Repair the toilet

Floors, flanges and slab homes

Many Desert Hot Springs homes are built on concrete slabs, so a leak at the base can soak into a subfloor or spread under tile. If the floor around the toilet feels soft or the grout is stained, say so on the call. Water that has been leaking for a while may need more than a new wax ring.

If the toilet is part of a bathroom update, see plumbing installation. For a toilet that keeps clogging even after repair, the cause may be in the line, which drain cleaning covers.

A replacement toilet has to meet current California water efficiency limits, and its installation follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), which is one reason old toilets are worth replacing.

Replacing a toilet, and what to expect

A toilet replacement takes about two hours when the flange is in good shape. We shut off the water, remove the old toilet, check the flange and the floor, set a new wax ring and tighten the bolts evenly. We then test the flush and check for leaks at the base and the supply line.

The flange is the part people forget. If it is cracked or sits below the finished floor, a new toilet will rock and leak. We repair the flange as part of the job and tell you what it adds to the price before we start.

Choosing a toilet for a desert home

Look for a model with a strong flush and a low water use rating. Comfort height seats are popular, and elongated bowls fit more adults. In a rental, a simple reliable design with parts that are easy to find saves repair visits. We can install the toilet you buy, or suggest a model if you ask.
