Plumber in Mountain View Country Estates, Desert Hot Springs, CA

Mountain View Country Estates is a residential community on the western edge of Desert Hot Springs. One local real estate guide puts its median build year in the late 1990s, which makes it a good place to talk about water heater and fixture age.

A home that is a few decades old has usually had its first set of parts replaced. The question is which ones are due next.

Where it sits

Wikipedia describes Mountain View Country Estates and Skyborne as newer master-planned communities on the western edge of Desert Hot Springs. Both are described as newer than the original townsite. See Skyborne for the community next door.

Next door is Mission Lakes Country Club, an older master-planned community that lies in unincorporated Riverside County, not inside the city. The two communities are neighbors, but they are not under the same local government, and that affects permits.

A note for Mission Lakes neighbors

If your address is in Mission Lakes Country Club, it is outside the city limits, so we are not treating it as part of this page. Call and ask whether we can help at your address.

Permits for county addresses go through Riverside County, not the City of Desert Hot Springs.

What one guide reports about the housing

A Palm Springs Life neighborhood table lists about 398 housing units here, about 73 percent of them owner-occupied, with a median build year of 1997. The figures come from aggregated data and should be read as approximate. We use them only to frame general advice.

When a repair is not enough

A leak from the body of a tank means the tank has failed and cannot be patched. See water heater replacement for what that involves, or water heater repair if the problem is a part.

If you see stains on a ceiling or damp spots on a wall, do not guess. Leak detection finds the source, and repiping is only worth discussing if leaks keep appearing in different places.

Drains and sewer

A single slow sink is usually local to that fixture. Several slow fixtures at once point to the main line. Drain cleaning handles the first case, and a camera inspection is the right step for the second.
